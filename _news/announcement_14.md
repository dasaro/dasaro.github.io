New arXiv preprint: [*An Unofficial FastLAS Tutorial: A Programmer's Guide*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23557), a hands-on introduction to FastLAS for students and users, drawn from earlier lectures. Every example is checked against FastLAS 2.2.0; the [task files are on GitHub](https://github.com/dasaro/fastlas_manual).
